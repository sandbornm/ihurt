# Neck and tennis case study

[← ihurt](../../README.md)

The project creator shared this case study for the README: stiffness in the left upper trap and high neck after sleeping on the side and stomach, followed by tennis serves. The note also records discomfort when lowering the chin toward the chest and turning left more than about 10 degrees. These are reported observations, not measurements or an assessment.

- [GIF preview](neck-tennis-case-study.gif): an 11-second excerpt showing pins, the note, and searches for exercise references.
- [Full video](neck-tennis-case-study.mp4): a 55-second, silent desktop recording at 1920 × 1080, with captions and anatomy credits.

The app runs in offline demo mode in this recording. Grok's controls appear, but no live Grok response or web search was recorded. Exercise links come from the curated source library, including NHS and UC Berkeley Health; they are general reading, not a personal exercise plan. The unedited symptom exports and recording scripts are not included here.

The GIF is trimmed from the original recording, reduced to 1120 pixels wide at 8 fps, and converted to a 128-color palette. The MP4 keeps the app footage and anatomy credit card, with a short section about provider setup removed.
